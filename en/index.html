What is RemindCat?

RemindCat is an app that remembers for you: when a subscription renews, when a bill is due, when a free trial ends, when you’re meeting friends. It’s all in one place, with a reminder before each date. No bank connection needed: open it and see what you owe this month.

Everything on one screen

Most of it is free.

Overview & calendar

See the next 7 days and how much is due this month; charge dates, due dates and events all on one calendar.

Subscription & bill reminders

Get reminded before you’re charged, with separate alerts for trial endings and cancel-by dates; bills keep reminding you until paid.

AI AssistantPRO

Ask “How much is due this month?”, or type one sentence and it becomes an event, to-do, subscription or bill.

Save moneyPRO

Price-change detection, a 12-month forecast, and duplicate subscriptions with how much you’d save.

Points wallet

Reminders before membership points expire.

Family sharing

Share events, to-dos and subscriptions with family. Free, up to 2 people.
